# Wheel & tire sets for street, strip and circuit

## What the game has today
- Wheels and tires are 4 separate part slots (wheels, rtires, fwheels,
  ftires). Swapping is 4 separate installs: 1 h of book time each (4 h for
  the lot), DIY or the shop (calendar.js `HOURS`).
- Wear travels with the part: a tire taken off keeps its wear on the shelf
  and brings it back when refitted (game.js, item.wear).
- Tunes: up to 10 named setup sheets per car (Strip tune / Street tune from
  the tuner); a pop-up after "To the lanes" picks one, free and instant.
  Circuit races use their own circuit setup. None of these touch parts.
- Getting there (legal.js): driving on slicks is a likely ticket ("Non-DOT
  racing slicks", $250); a truck & trailer ($8,500) or a flatbed avoids it.
- Backlog: shelf tires are per axle (a front tire can't go on a rear).

## Real world (research)
- **Two sets is normal.** Street cars that race usually run a second set of
  wheels: a "drag pack" (skinny front runners + drag radials or slicks on
  the rear, ~77 lb lighter on one C6 Corvette) or a track-day set on
  R-compounds. Track-day guides: a second set pays for itself at 3-5 track
  days a year. (Tire Rack "track day tires 101", Corvette Forum drag pack
  threads, Rennlist/s2ki.)
- **Swap at the track.** Drive there on street tires, carry the race set in
  the hatch, swap in the pits with a jack ("throw them in the hatch and swap
  them at the track", Stangnet). Slicks aren't DOT legal ("competition use
  only", Hoosier/Jegs); DOT drag radials can be driven there in the dry.
  Front runners are unsafe on the street (can't brake hard, can break in a
  corner).
- **How long:** a DIY four-wheel swap with a floor jack and an impact gun
  is about 20-40 min (forum reports 20, 30, 35-40 min); a pro ~15 min.
  Mounting and balancing tires on wheels is the longer job.
- **Drag & drive (Hot Rod Drag Week):** everything has to be carried in the
  car or a small trailer pulled by it (U-Haul 4x8 size limit); racers swap
  to slicks at each track and back to street tires for the drive.

## Other games
- **Gran Turismo** (GT6 manual via search; GT7 guides): settings sheets
  (A/B/C in GT6, several in GT7) hold the parts choice including the tires;
  you buy a part once and any sheet can use it; switch sheets in the garage
  or before a race. Closest match to the idea. (gran-turismo.com itself is
  blocked by this session's network; read through search summaries.)
- **Forza Motorsport (2023):** parts can be removed and refitted freely;
  no named build slots found.
- No Limit Drag Racing 2 / CSR2: no set presets found.

## Options (sent to the owner)
1. **What a set is:** named wheel & tire set for all 4 corners (Street,
   Strip, Circuit...), made from parts you own.
2. **Swapping a set:** one job, ~0.5 h (real 20-40 min), not 4 h.
3. **Where you can swap:** garage only / at the track too (carry it there).
4. **Automatic or picked:** each set tagged for street/strip/circuit and
   fitted automatically when you race there, or picked in the pre-race
   pop-up.
5. **Link to tunes:** a setup sheet can bring its set (Strip tune = slicks).
6. **Carrying:** driving the car carries one spare set; truck & trailer
   carries any.
7. Rivals unchanged.
